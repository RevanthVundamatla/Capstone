How the agent works

State

Each degraded UIEB frame — its color histogram, contrast and haze estimate — becomes the observation the policy reads.

Action

DDPG outputs a continuous correction vector: white-balance shift, contrast stretch, and dehazing strength.

Reward

The corrected frame is scored against its reference on PSNR, SSIM, UIQM and UCIQE; the blended score is the reward signal.

Policy

An actor-critic pair refines the correction policy over training episodes until scores plateau on the held-out split.

Why these four metrics

Same dataset, same metrics, same problem statement across the team — only the restoration algorithm changes per person.

PSNR
Pixel-level fidelity to the reference image, in decibels — higher means less distortion.
SSIM
Structural similarity to the reference — how well edges, texture and luminance are preserved.
UIQM
Underwater Image Quality Measure — a no-reference score combining colorfulness, sharpness and contrast.
UCIQE
Underwater Color Image Quality Evaluation — weights chroma, saturation and luminance variance for underwater scenes.
