Image quality evaluation

Understanding the metrics

Different metrics measure different aspects of image quality and should be interpreted together.

PSNR

Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio measures reconstruction error relative to a reference. Higher values generally indicate lower error.

SSIM

Structural Similarity compares luminance, contrast, and structure between an image and its reference.

UIQM and UCIQE

These no-reference metrics estimate aspects of underwater image quality. Higher values alone do not guarantee better perceived quality.
